Four steps the jobs-api page describes

  1. Find and rank open roles on employer boards. The page names Greenhouse, Lever, and Ashby in the walkthrough, and also SmartRecruiters and Workable in the catalog. Jev scores fit against a resume.
  2. Find the hiring manager. The page’s words: “the team that owns the role, not the recruiter.”
  3. Write a short pitch to that person. The agent writes the note.
  4. Check salary. Percentiles are one call. A full benchmark report is another.

The rate card

Rates listed on treg.to as of Nov 22, 2026; may change.

  • Job search — $0.0015 per job
  • New roles since last check — $0.002 per new job
  • Closed roles — free
  • Salary percentiles — $0.01 per call
  • Full salary benchmark report — $0.25 per report
  • Jev fit score — billed by input tokens. This page uses an illustrative $0.50 per 1,000,000 input tokens. Illustrative price invented for this page. Not a Jev price.

The jobs-api page also says an empty page costs nothing, and that new teams start with $1.00 free. It names people.search and people.email.find and does not print a price beside them. The people catalog lists routed people.search as free and treg.people.email.find at $0.00095 per success. This meter uses those catalog figures for the hiring-manager lines, and it assumes each email lookup succeeds.

Where the money goes

A typical hunt on this page, about 20 searches and no benchmark report, lands around a few cents. One $0.25 report costs the same neighborhood as 167 searches at $0.0015. Thousands of those cheap searches are what it takes before search outspends a single report: 2,000 searches are $3.00.

Harbor & Pine’s posting

The role is Transaction Coordinator. Flip the posting, then watch the shortlist. A named owner does not change the sketch fit. It changes who the note is addressed to. Pay and the board change whether the post is in the list at all.
