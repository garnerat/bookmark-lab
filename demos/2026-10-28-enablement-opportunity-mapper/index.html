Rank the work they would actually adopt

You are the facilitator. Mill Creek Mechanical is a fictional 14-person HVAC and plumbing shop in Cincinnati. Step through the kickoff and each department. Every session ends with tasks people would hand to AI tomorrow. The map scores those tasks by adopted hours, not by the raw number.

The idea is from Luke Pierce’s article “AI Audits Are Dead. Sell This Instead,” which this bookmarked post points at. This page is a made-up example of that framing. It is not a pitch for a program, and it is not that article’s clients.

All names, hours, and notes are fictional example data. Mill Creek Mechanical is not a real company. Jobline is not a real product. Nothing is uploaded, and the page does not call a model.

What the owner is actually buying

Audit-first

Paid for up front
A standalone audit. The document is the product.
What week one feels like
Questions, a tool inventory, and a wait for the writeup.
What gets gathered
Process steps and hours, often as a manager remembers them.
What usually gets missed
Who is curious and who is resistant, and which workflows people would actually adopt. High hours get treated as high value.
What they leave with
The audit. A build is a second sale, with hope that people will change how they work.

Enablement-first

Paid for up front
A leadership kickoff, then a hands-on session with each department.
What week one feels like
People in the room, trying a draft on a real task of theirs before the hour is over.
What gets gathered
At the end of every session, each person lists tasks they would hand to AI tomorrow, with hours and how often. The facilitator notes curious, mixed, or resistant, and which workflows they would adopt a tool for.
What usually gets missed
Less of the adoption signal. That signal is the rank. Discovery is a side effect of the training, not a separate phase.
What they leave with
The opportunity map, walked through live, then a build proposal that already includes monthly enablement. Adoption is treated as the predictor of whether a build gets used.

Opportunity map and build proposal

Plain text, from the sessions you have opened and any edits you made. It names the top builds and keeps a monthly enablement session in each one.
