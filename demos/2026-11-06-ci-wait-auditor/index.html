Audit a 40-minute CI wait

Oakley Heating & Cooling is a made-up shop in Oakley, Cincinnati. Its agents push the oakley-ops repo all day. This page is one pull request and the check that pull request waits on. The bar splits every job into waiting, setup, and running.

From Owen Gretzinger’s post and his Boardy write-up, How I made our CI 20x faster. A year of agent-written pull requests had been waiting about 40 minutes. After the levers below, a run waited about 2. The figures on this page are a sketch of that shape for a smaller shop repo, not Boardy’s logs.

Oakley Heating & Cooling is fictional. The 40:00 start, the 11:00 runner queue, and every other second are example numbers. Nothing is uploaded. The page does not call a network.
