Agent Spend Ledger

Tallgrass Studio is a fictional four-person web design and marketing shop in Cincinnati. Priya, Owen, Maya, and Luis are fictional too. They use coding and writing agents on client sites, proposals, and the studio’s own admin.

Andrew bookmarked Claire Vo (@clairevo), 9 Oct 2026:

I can tell you where every penny of token spend is going against my business, can you?

This whole analysis cost me $0.12

Here's how I track everything my agents are doing to tie AI use to business impact

In the thread she reads Codex and Claude Code session logs and Cursor’s local database, then pulls cloud-agent records, GitHub pull requests, and billing invoices. Embeddings propose pairs. A small model (she names Jev) judges whether a pair is the same workstream. The kept links become a graph, the graph is clustered, and a model (she names Gemini) names the groups. Usage is then cut four ways: tokens, estimated cost, real cost, and session hours, by business line, week, tool, or model. She later says estimated value means the API list price of the tokens, as against what was actually paid. Figures she reports include a launch around $4k, billing admin around $20 a month, about $1.5k a month paid for about $5k a month of list-price tokens, half of pull requests as singletons, and $0.12 to run the analysis.

This page does not run embeddings, a judge model, or Gemini. The similarity scores and the yes-or-no judge marks are prepared. Tallgrass is a smaller shop than the one in the thread, and every number here is made up.

Illustrative, fictional. Hearth & Rye Bakery, Keller & Moss Law, and Riverbend Youth Soccer are not real clients. The sessions, pull requests, and invoices were written for this page. Stand-in list rates, not a price card: Composer and Sonnet $15 / $75 per million tokens in and out, Haiku $1 / $5, Codex $2 / $10, Draft small $0.80 / $4. Cursor is 2 seats × $40 a month.
