Inbox triage that never leaves your browser

Queen City Comfort is a fictional Cincinnati HVAC and plumbing shop. This page loads a small open instruct model in the tab and turns each email into a fixed JSON record: a summary, action items, events, and a priority of high, medium, or low.

The idea is borrowed from @barronnotbaron on X. They ran Ternary Bonsai 2 4B locally on a MacBook, 32 streams at once, turning 250 emails into JSON (summary, action items, events, priority) at 700+ tokens/sec in under 4 GB. High-volume, low-stakes extraction can stay on hardware you already own. This demo is that job shrunk to one browser tab and a 0.5B model, so the drafts are rougher and they run one at a time.

Nothing is sent to any server after the model download. Sample emails and anything you paste are processed in this tab only. There is no backend and no API key.

The first visit downloads about 490 MB when WebGPU is available (461 MB of q4f16 weights, a 7 MB tokenizer, and a 21 MB ONNX runtime) or about 780 MB for the WASM fallback (750 MB of q4 weights, plus the same tokenizer and runtime). The browser caches those files after that.

Model: onnx-community/Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct via Transformers.js. WebGPU when this browser can use it, otherwise WASM.

The model stays unloaded until you press Load model.
